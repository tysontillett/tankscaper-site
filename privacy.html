Privacy policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

Tankscaper is a game made by a small independent team in Perth, Western Australia. This page explains what information the game uses and why.

  • Your game is saved on your own device. It isn't sent to us.
  • You don't need an account, and we never ask for your name, email or location.
  • The game has no ads.
  • We don't sell your information to anyone.

What the game stores

Your progress (your tanks, fish, coins, gems, level and settings) is saved on your device so you can pick up where you left off. It is not sent to us. If you delete the app, this saved progress is deleted with it.

The name and avatar you can choose in the game stay on your device too.

Ads

Tankscaper doesn't show ads. If ads are ever added, this policy will be updated before they appear, and it will explain what they collect.

Purchases

Any purchase in the game is handled by Apple through the App Store, or by Google through Google Play. We never see or store your payment details. The store only tells the game that a purchase was made, so it can give you what you bought.

Crash reports

If you have turned on sharing with app developers in your phone's settings, Apple or Google may send us anonymous crash reports. These help us fix bugs and don't identify you.

If you email us

If you contact us, we use your email address and message only to reply and help with your question. We don't add you to a mailing list unless you ask us to.

This website

This website doesn't use cookies, analytics or ads, and it doesn't load anything from other companies. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which keeps the IP addresses of visitors for security purposes, as most web hosts do.

Children

Tankscaper is made for everyone, including children. The game doesn't collect personal information from anyone, children included. Purchases are handled by Apple and Google, whose own parental controls apply. If you believe a child has emailed us personal information, let us know and we will delete it.

Your rights

We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles. Because the game keeps your progress on your device, we hold almost nothing about you. If you have emailed us, you can ask to see or delete what we hold, and we will do it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@tankscapergame.com

Tankscaper is made in Perth, Western Australia.
